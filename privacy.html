Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 10, 2026

At Lucknow Car Towing Crane Service (https://towingcraneservice.shop), your privacy is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect and how we safeguard it when you contact our 24x7 roadside helpline.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact our roadside dispatch team via phone call, SMS, or WhatsApp, we may collect the following details strictly for emergency dispatch purposes:

  • Your contact phone number and name.
  • Your breakdown location or GPS pin in Lucknow.
  • Vehicle model, registration type, and nature of the breakdown.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Dispatch our nearest towing crane or flatbed driver to your location.
  • Coordinate roadside assistance and confirm delivery to your preferred mechanic or residence.
  • Provide customer service and follow up on completed towing trips.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third parties or marketing agencies. Your location and contact information are shared only with the assigned crane driver or recovery technician handling your service call.

4. Contact Information
